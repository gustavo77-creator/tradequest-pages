Managing your subscription

Subscriptions are billed and managed through your Apple ID, not inside the App. To cancel or change your plan, go to your device's Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions.

Resetting your progress

Open the Progress tab, tap the gear icon in the top right, and choose "Reset All Progress." This clears your session history, buying power, and badges and can't be undone.

Something not working?
