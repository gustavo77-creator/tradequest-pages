Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

TradeQuest ("the App") is a trading-judgment training simulator. This policy explains what information the App collects and how it's handled.

The short version

TradeQuest does not have a server, and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information off of your device. Everything the App tracks — your simulated buying power, trade history, scores, badges, and the display name you optionally set — is stored locally on your device only, using standard device storage. We never see it, and it is never sent anywhere.

Account & sign-in

TradeQuest does not require creating an account or signing in. Any "trader name" you enter is used only to personalize your own device — it is not transmitted to us or shared with anyone.

Subscriptions & payment

If you purchase a subscription, that purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details, card numbers, or billing address — that information is handled by Apple under Apple's own Privacy Policy.

Analytics & tracking

TradeQuest does not currently use any analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We don't know how many people use the App, how often, or how they use it. If this ever changes, this policy will be updated first.

Children's privacy

TradeQuest is not directed at children and is not designed to collect information from anyone. Since no personal information is collected from any user, none is knowingly collected from children either.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored only on your device, you're always in control of it. You can erase all of your TradeQuest data at any time using "Reset All Progress" in the app's Settings, or by deleting the app from your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change and the updated policy will be posted at this same address.
