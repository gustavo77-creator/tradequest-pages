Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TradeQuest ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the TradeQuest mobile application ("the App"). We built TradeQuest to require as little personal information as possible.

1 — Information We Collect

  • No account required. TradeQuest does not require you to sign in or create an account, and we do not collect your name, email, or other identifying information directly within the App.
  • Purchase information. If you subscribe to TradeQuest Pro, your payment is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not see or store your payment details (such as card numbers). We receive only subscription status information (e.g., whether you have an active subscription) needed to unlock Pro features, via our subscription management provider, RevenueCat.
  • Usage and device data. The App and its service providers may automatically collect limited technical data, such as device type, operating system version, and app usage/crash diagnostics, to help us keep the App working correctly.

2 — How We Use Information

We use the information above only to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's features
  • Verify and manage your TradeQuest Pro subscription
  • Diagnose and fix technical issues
  • Improve the App over time

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3 — Third-Party Services

TradeQuest relies on the following third-party services, which process limited data on our behalf:

  • Apple App Store — processes all payments and manages your subscription billing. Subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription status and entitlements. Subject to RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

4 — Data Retention

We retain the minimal information described above only as long as needed to provide the App's features or as required by law.

5 — Children's Privacy

TradeQuest is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6 — Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete information associated with you. Since TradeQuest does not maintain user accounts or directly store personal identifiers, most requests can be resolved by contacting us directly (see below) or managing your subscription through your Apple ID account settings.

7 — Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
